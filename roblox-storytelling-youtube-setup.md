## 2. Branding briefs
- **Avatar (800x800):** a Roblox-style character holding a glowing storybook, bright background, readable at tiny size.
- **Banner (2048x1152, keep text inside the center 1235x338):** "New Roblox Stories Every Week" plus the upload schedule.
- **Thumbnail style:** 1 expressive character close-up, 3-4 words max in big bold text, high contrast, one bright accent color. Use the same layout every time.
- Make these in Canva (free). I can generate them if you ask.

## 3. Content format
**Shorts (under 60 s)**
- Hook in the first 2 seconds: "He had no idea the admin was watching…"
- 3-beat story: setup > twist > cliffhanger or payoff.
- Big captions on screen, voiceover or text-to-speech, vertical 9:16.
- End with: "Part 2? Comment YES."

**Long videos (5-10 min):** full stories, with Shorts cut from them as teasers.

**Story genres that work:** Brookhaven RP dramas, Adopt Me trades gone wrong, "Oh no" scams and rescues, mystery and horror-lite (keep it kid-safe), revenge or karma stories.

## 4. Short script template
```
HOOK (0-2s):   one shocking/curious line
SETUP (2-15s): who, where, what they wanted
TWIST (15-40s): what went wrong / surprise
PAYOFF (40-55s): result + cliffhanger
CTA (last 3s): "Follow for Part 2"
```

## 5. Production workflow (free tools)
1. Write the script (I can write them for you).
2. Record gameplay: Roblox's built-in recorder or OBS Studio.
3. Voiceover: your own mic (Audacity) or a text-to-speech tool.
4. Edit: CapCut (captions, auto-subtitles) or DaVinci Resolve.
5. Export 1080x1920 for Shorts.

## 6. Rules to follow
- Roblox permits monetized gameplay videos under its Brand Guidelines. Check them before monetizing.
- Use only copyright-safe music (YouTube Audio Library).
- Keep content kid-safe: no gore, no real-world scares, no personal info.
- Don't copy other creators' stories. Write original ones or credit/ask for permission.
- Disclose any AI voice or AI-generated content as YouTube requires.
- Monetization (YPP): 500 subs + 3 public uploads + 3,000 watch hours or 3M Shorts views.

## 10. Reference style: itsRedBear (from a screenshot, not a full review)
Observed on the channel page: 55.7k subs, 21 videos, Shorts at roughly 282k to 12M views each.

What to copy as a *format* (do not copy their stories, name, mascot or art):
- **Recurring mascot character.** One recognizable avatar (theirs wears a red bear hat) appears in many videos. Make your own original mascot, for example a storybook-themed avatar, and use it as the channel avatar too.
- **Big word-by-word captions.** One to three bold words on screen at a time ("share", "no", "power", "Blocks fall from"), centered, with an outline. Do this in CapCut using auto-captions with a big font.
- **Bright, saturated Roblox scenes** with the character large and centered in a vertical 9:16 frame, so the thumbnail frame works at tiny size.
- **Mix of avatars.** Anime-style and cute avatars in different outfits, so every video looks distinct but stays on-brand.
- **Fast, high-volume Shorts.** Short stories or "what if" scenarios told in seconds, which suits Shorts.
- **Engagement hooks in the bio.** "comment for a cookie" gives viewers a reason to comment. Make your own version, such as "comment a ⭐ for Part 2".
- **Channel Join (memberships)** button, which is available once you qualify for monetization.

Takeaway: the shortest path to this look is one original mascot + loud word-by-word captions + bright maps + a 20-40 s story with a twist, posted several times a week.
